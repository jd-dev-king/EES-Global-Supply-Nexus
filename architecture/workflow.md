# Procure-to-Production Workflow

1. Purchase requisition is created and approved.
2. A purchase order is placed with a qualified supplier.
3. Materials are received into quarantine.
4. Incoming inspection verifies count, condition, CoA, identity, and specification alignment.
5. Quality disposition releases or blocks the lot.
6. Released lots are allocated to scheduled manufacturing batches.
7. A manufacturing handoff record transmits readiness, lots, timing, and constraints to Pharma Process Twin.
